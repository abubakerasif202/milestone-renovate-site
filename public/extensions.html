EXTENSIONS & ADDITIONS

Expanding your footprint with architectural intent.

An extension should feel like a natural evolution of your home, not an awkward afterthought.

Whether you require an expansive open-plan ground floor rear addition opening to a level lawn, or a second-storey addition creating a private parents’ retreat, our team focuses on architectural cohesion and structural integrity.

We guide you through the regulatory landscape across Sydney councils—working out whether complying development (CDC) under the Codes SEPP may suit your site, or preparing a Development Application (DA) where heritage or site constraints require it.
