GOOD QUESTIONS, GOOD STARTS

Wondering where
to begin?

Every home is different. Here are a few starting points worth considering.

Can we renovate while living in the home?

It depends on the scope. Some works can be staged while occupied, while significant renovation may require temporary arrangements. We can discuss practicality during planning.

Do renovation projects require approvals?

Some works may need council, strata or other approvals. Requirements depend on the location and nature of the work, especially structural or external changes.

Can you help with planning and finishes?

Yes, the enquiry process can start with your layout priorities, design preferences, material ideas and practical buildability questions.

What should I prepare before getting in touch?

Share your suburb, type of space, approximate budget and desired timeframe. Reference photos or a simple wish list can help start the conversation.
