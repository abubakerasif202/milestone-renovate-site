COST PLANNER

Cost estimate disclaimer.

Please read this before relying on any estimate from this website.

Effective 12 October 2026. Pricing information last reviewed 12 October 2026.

What the calculator shows

The bathroom calculator produces an indicative planning range in Australian dollars and weeks using fixed adjustments described on the calculator page. The separate kitchen guide uses published Sydney market benchmarks. Neither is a Milestone Renovate quotation.

It is not a quote or a contract

An estimate from this website is not a quotation, an offer, a fixed price or a contract, and it does not oblige us or you to do anything. A binding price can only be given in a written quotation or contract prepared after we have assessed your property, plans, selections and site conditions. Quotations and building contracts are not issued by this website. They are issued by the contracting entity responsible for the work, which is named, together with its licence details, in the written quotation or contract you receive. Residential building work in New South Wales requires a written contract above $5,000 (including GST). See NSW Government guidance.

What affects the real cost

Actual costs depend on inspection, plans and engineering, product and finish selections, access, the age and condition of the building, hidden conditions found once walls and floors are opened, approvals, supplier lead times and the market at the time of the work. Your final cost can be lower or higher than the range shown.

GST, inclusions and exclusions

The bathroom calculator does not assert GST treatment: the written quote confirms tax treatment and inclusions. Its labour-only package assumes you source materials; all-inclusive adds the stated materials allowance. Published kitchen market ranges include GST. They do not include design or engineering fees, council or certifier fees, asbestos or hazardous material removal, structural or services upgrades beyond those you select, appliances for kitchens, furniture, or a contingency allowance for hidden conditions unless the calculator states otherwise. Allow a contingency on top of the range for older properties.

Timelines

Timelines are indicative planning ranges and exclude approvals and product lead times unless stated. Programme dates are confirmed in a written contract.

Your consumer rights

This disclaimer does not exclude, restrict or modify any right or remedy you have under the Australian Consumer Law or any other law that cannot lawfully be excluded. We do not make misleading claims about price: if you are given a written quotation, that document sets out the price and scope.
