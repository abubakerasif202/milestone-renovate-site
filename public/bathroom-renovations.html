BATHROOM RENOVATIONS

Serenity engineered with rigorous technical detail.

A successful bathroom renovation blends restorative luxury with exacting wet-area construction.

From heritage Inner West terrace bathrooms with tight footprints to expansive North Shore ensuites, spatial proportion and waterproofing integrity are paramount. We carefully design falls to waste, recessed shower niches, and in-wall cisterns to maximize usable space.

Every detail is planned in advance—from AS 3740 waterproofing membranes and acoustic waste isolation to balanced ventilation that prevents moisture build-up and preserves pristine tile and stone surfaces.
