How this planning guide works

Floor area is length × width, or the known area you enter. The profile combines unitless scope points for size, renovation type, finish level, plumbing and site considerations. Additional selections highlight practical work and uncertainty. It is a conversation aid, not an engineering assessment.

Compare retaining a layout or simplifying finishes while keeping essential site and accessibility requirements. These alternatives indicate scope changes; they do not promise a saving or override waterproofing, structural or accessibility needs.

What still needs review

Concealed damage, hazardous materials, structural engineering, approvals, product availability, trade sequencing and access arrangements cannot be established here. Your preferred start date is a preference, not a construction programme.
