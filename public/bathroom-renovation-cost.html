HOW THE ESTIMATE WORKS

Transparent by design.
Here is the arithmetic.

These ranges are indicative Sydney market benchmarks compiled from published 2026 cost guides. They are not Milestone Renovate prices or quotes. Pricing last reviewed 12 October 2026 (version 2026-10-12).

1. Base range per finish level

Each base range is the equal-weight average of the published lows and of the published highs from two independent Sydney sources: an aggregator of marketplace and trade data and a Sydney builder’s guide. They differ materially, so we do not lean on either. All figures include GST.

Published figure means the number comes from, or is derived from, a cited source. Planning allowance means no published figure exists and Milestone has set a cautious allowance that will be calibrated against real quotes. Final results are rounded down (low) and up (high) to the nearest $500.

4. Timelines

Bathroom: 3–6 weeks on site, with extra lead time for strata approval or custom-order items. Kitchen: 2–3 weeks on site and 10–14 weeks from design sign-off to completion because cabinetry is manufactured to order.

5. What is not estimated online

  • Whole-home renovations vary too widely for a reliable online range (only one published Sydney figure was found).
  • No published Sydney extension pricing was found that is specific enough to estimate responsibly.
  • Labour-only pricing is not estimated; if you supply your own fixtures we quote labour separately.
