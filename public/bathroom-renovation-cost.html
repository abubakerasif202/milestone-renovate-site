How this works

  • Area is length × width. It helps place your bathroom in a size band.
  • Each choice adds a relative scope point: renovation type, finish level, plumbing changes, size and site considerations. These are unitless planning points, not prices.
  • Points are grouped into a light, moderate, substantial or complex scope profile.
  • When Milestone approves a rate card, this tool can show an indicative range. Until then no figure is shown, on purpose.
